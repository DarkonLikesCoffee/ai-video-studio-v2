# Round 1 — aesthetic review

1. **Weak depth and spatial layering (highest impact):** the keeper, tower, coast, and sky read as flat layers with little atmospheric perspective.
2. **Stiff keeper performance:** the keeper mostly slides across the frame; the walk and struggle need body rhythm and grounded footwork.
3. **Dull storm lighting:** the storm palette is consistently dark but has too little directional contrast or lightning shaping the silhouette.

Reference gap: compared with MadridTrip's dimensional camera/map staging and TinyAstronaut's layered 3D lighting, LighthouseStorm reads like a set of flat vector cutouts.

Technical issues: none observed in the first source review; render/typecheck verification is recorded by the run.
