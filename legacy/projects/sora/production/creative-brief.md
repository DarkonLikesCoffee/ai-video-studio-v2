# SORA — Product Film

## Objective
Introduce a fictional premium over-ear headphone through form, tactile detail and a memorable hero reveal. This is a visual benchmark, not a real product listing.

## Audience and response
Design-conscious listeners should perceive the object as precise, calm and premium, and remember its silhouette.

## Creative thesis
Sound, shaped around you. Reveal the headphones in four deliberate stages: material, silhouette, interface detail, complete object.

## Art direction
Warm mineral studio: near-black graphite, muted ivory, copper-orange accent. Large editorial typography, generous negative space, hard directional highlights and restrained grain. No HUDs, particles or generic tech motifs.

## Motion and edit
Macro push, decisive match cut to a three-quarter hero, lateral detail move, then a slow settle into a still final frame. Hard cuts are motivated by shape and highlight continuity.

## Sound
Original minimal electronic pulse with soft material Foley and one restrained closing impact. No voice-over or factual product claims.

## Assumptions and rights
SORA is fictional. All visual artwork is original vector illustration. No real specifications, endorsements or brand affiliations are implied.

## Acceptance criteria
- Four visually distinct shot scales and compositions.
- Headphone silhouette is coherent across all shots.
- Copy remains legible and inside safe areas.
- Every shot changes visually with purposeful motion.
- Final hero frame holds clearly and resolves the opening.
