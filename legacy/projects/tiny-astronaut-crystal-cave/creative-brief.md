# The Last Light — Creative Brief

## Concept
A lone, tiny astronaut crosses a rust-coloured alien world, follows a faint cyan glow into an ancient cavern, and discovers a towering crystal that awakens as they approach.

## Delivery
- 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds (600 frames)
- Silent visual short; no text, subtitles, narration or music
- One dedicated custom Remotion composition: TinyAstronautCrystalCave

## Visual direction
- Alien surface: dusty mauve and burnt umber terrain, a huge distant planet and two small moons
- Explorer: tiny ivory pressure suit with dark reflective visor and warm orange-red backpack details
- Cave: deep violet stone, jagged rock silhouettes, a few embedded cyan crystal shards
- Discovery: monumental multi-faceted cyan crystal, animated internal glow and floating motes
- Cinematic camera: establish, follow, push into cave, reveal and restrained final pull-back
- Lighting contrast shifts from warm low sun to cool crystal spill

## Story beats
1. 0–3.5s: the lander descends onto the alien plain.
2. 3.5–8.5s: the astronaut leaves the lander and traverses the terrain.
3. 8.5–12.5s: the explorer approaches the cave; warm light gives way to darkness.
4. 12.5–16.5s: the camera enters the cavern and finds the enormous crystal.
5. 16.5–20s: the astronaut approaches; the crystal pulses brighter and fills the cave with cyan light.

## Acceptance criteria
- Only this new composition is requested for rendering.
- The astronaut is clearly readable as a small explorer, not a generic floating shape.
- The planet landing, approach, cave entrance and crystal reveal are visibly distinct beats.
- Camera and character/environment motion continue meaningfully throughout the 20 seconds.
- No text or editorial overlays appear.
- The crystal awakening visibly changes the cave lighting and particle atmosphere.
- The exact MP4 is inspected before final acceptance.
