# Storyboard — The Last Light

| Time | Beat | Framing / camera | Visual event | Motion / transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:03.5 | Arrival | Wide, slightly elevated | A compact lander drops onto the plain beneath the distant planet | Lander descends; dust and surface motes drift; camera glides lower |
| 00:03.5–00:08.5 | Exploration | Medium-wide tracking | Astronaut exits the landing area and crosses foreground terrain | Legs/arms stride, body bobs; camera tracks laterally and advances |
| 00:08.5–00:12.5 | Threshold | Low over-shoulder | Explorer approaches the black mouth of a rock-cut cave | Camera pushes toward cave; warm landscape falls away |
| 00:12.5–00:16.5 | Discovery | Deep wide reveal | Cavern opens around a towering crystal, tiny astronaut held against its scale | Camera arcs inward; crystal glows, fragments/motes drift |
| 00:16.5–00:20 | Awakening | Medium-wide, then pull-back | Astronaut nears the core; crystal flares and throws light over the cavern | Crystal pulses; particles rise; cool spill grows; camera retreats to hold final image |

## Source-to-shot implementation
- PlanetScene: landing pod, distant planet/moons, procedural terrain and astronaut.
- DarkCaveScene + Cave: cave shell, floor, stalactites, embedded shards and dust motes.
- Crystal: central faceted crystal; emission and point lights pulse during awakening.
- TinyAstronautCrystalCave: camera path, phase timing, vignette and shot continuity.

## Continuity audit
One astronaut and one design language throughout. Exterior terrain and warm light are confined to the first half; the cave and cyan crystal carry the second half. No repeated card layout, text overlays or unrelated inserts.
