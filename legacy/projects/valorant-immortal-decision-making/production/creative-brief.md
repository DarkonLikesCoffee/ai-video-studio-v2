# Creative brief — The Difference Between Diamond and Immortal

## Communication goal
Show Diamond players that aim is only one part of competitive success: Immortal-level consistency comes from decisions that increase the team's chance of winning the round.

## Audience
VALORANT players around Diamond who are mechanically capable but want to understand what changes at higher ranks.

## Creative thesis
**Winning aim duels isn't enough. The difference is decision-making.**

The edit contrasts the isolated duel with the coordinated round. Red represents threat and unnecessary risk; blue represents team information, spacing and control. The piece should feel like an esports documentary, not an in-game montage.

## Visual system
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 60-second target.
- Near-black backgrounds, VALORANT-inspired red, white typography and restrained blue information accents.
- Editorial headlines on the left; original tactical diagrams on the right.
- Moving route lines, shifting markers, target geometry, warning zones, particle accents and kinetic typography.
- Hard cuts and match cuts; avoid repetitive wipes and static title cards.

## Audio
- US English Kokoro narration; narration is the timing master.
- Original generated electronic score at a restrained level with sidechain ducking.
- Original transition whooshes and low impact/chime accents.
- Word-aligned subtitles derived from the generated narration alignment.

## Footage and rights
No authentic VALORANT gameplay clips were supplied. Therefore, this version uses clearly illustrative original vector tactical visualizations and does not imitate official game footage, agents, HUD, maps or logos. No third-party music or gameplay is included.

## Acceptance
The final MP4 must have purposeful motion in every chapter, readable synchronized captions, clear narration, balanced audio, varied shot grammar, and a resolved final message. A green render is not sufficient; inspect the actual MP4 before calling it complete.
